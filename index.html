Who's Lucas?

Lucas is our wild-haired guide to games that don't always explain themselves. He gets lost, presses the wrong button, and occasionally discovers something useful.

Father Knows Less is about sharing those discoveries—especially in lesser-known and early-access games—so the next player has a slightly easier time.
